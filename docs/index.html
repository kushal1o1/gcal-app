How it works.

Three steps, one spoken sentence.

1

Speak

Tap the mic and say what you need, in plain words.

2

Check

The preview shows the time, and whether it is urgent or repeats.

3

Create

The event goes into Google Calendar. Google sends the alert on time.

Made to stay out of the way.

Small on purpose. Everything else is left to Google Calendar.

Urgent reminders

Say “urgent” or “important” and you get an email and a popup, a day and an hour before, and at the time.

Repeats

“Every month”, “daily”, or “every 2 weeks” creates a recurring event.

Upcoming list

See what is next, filter by all, recurring, critical, or normal, and edit reminders or delete them in bulk.

Home screen widget

Your next three reminders, right on the home screen.

  1. Download the file that matches your phone.
  2. Allow installs from your browser when Android asks.
  3. Open gcal, go to Settings, and enter your server address and token.

Bring your own server.

gcal sends your words to a small server that creates the calendar event. The server is open source, and you can run your own copy.
